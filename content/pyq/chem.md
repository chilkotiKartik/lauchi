## 1. Syllabus Topics
* **Spectroscopy:** Principles of spectroscopy, electromagnetic radiation spectrum, selection rules, Born-Oppenheimer approximation[cite: 1].
* **Electronic (UV-Visible) Spectroscopy:** Types of electronic transitions ($\sigma \to \sigma^*, n \to \sigma^*, \pi \to \pi^*, n \to \pi^*$), chromophores, auxochromes, absorption shifts (bathochromic, hypsochromic, hyperchromic, hypochromic), Beer-Lambert Law and applications[cite: 1, 19, 23, 27, 29, 30, 35].
* **Vibrational (IR) Spectroscopy:** Principle, molecular vibrations (stretching: symmetric/asymmetric; bending: scissoring, rocking, wagging, twisting), selection rule ($\frac{d\mu}{dx} \neq 0$), calculation of vibrational degrees of freedom ($3N-6$ and $3N-5$), and importance of the fingerprint region[cite: 1, 19, 20, 22, 23, 25, 27, 35].

* **Nuclear Magnetic Resonance ($^1\text{H}$ NMR) Spectroscopy & MRI:** Principle, nuclear spin states, chemical shift ($\delta$ scale), shielding and deshielding mechanisms, spin-spin coupling and coupling constant, TMS as internal reference, structure elucidation of organic molecules, basic principle of Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)[cite: 1, 19, 20, 23, 25, 27, 28, 29, 35].
* **Basic Organic Reaction Mechanisms:** Addition reactions (electrophilic and nucleophilic addition), Substitution reactions ($S_N1$ and $S_N2$ mechanisms with kinetics and stereochemistry: Walden inversion vs. racemization), Elimination reactions, Cyclization reactions (Diels-Alder $[4+2]$ cycloaddition)[cite: 1, 19, 20, 22, 25, 27, 28, 30].
* **Drug Synthesis:** Complete synthesis pathways, reaction conditions, and pharmaceutical uses of: Aspirin, Phenacetin, Melubrin, or Novalgin[cite: 1, 20].
