# UNIT I: SEMICONDUCTOR MATERIALS & JUNCTION DIODE

## 1. Syllabus Topics
* **Semiconductor Materials & Properties:** Group-IV materials, covalent bonds, basic energy band concepts in materials (valence band, conduction band, forbidden energy gap)[cite: 1, 63, 65, 66].
* Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, donor and acceptor impurities, law of mass action ($n \cdot p = n_i^2$), carrier transport: drift current, diffusion current, mobility ($\mu$), conductivity of semiconductors ($\sigma = q(n\mu_n + p\mu_p)$), and concept of Fermi level[cite: 1, 63, 64, 65, 66].
* **Junction Diode:** P-N junction formation, depletion layer, built-in barrier potential, forward and reverse bias terminal behavior, $V\text{-}I$ characteristics, Shockley diode equation, static resistance ($R_{dc}$) and dynamic resistance ($r_{ac}$), diode transition and diffusion capacitances[cite: 1, 63, 64].

## 1. Syllabus Topics
* **Diode Applications:** Half-Wave Rectifier (HWR), Full-Wave Rectifiers: Centre-Tapped and Bridge Rectifier (circuit operation, input/output waveforms, average DC current/voltage, RMS current/voltage)[cite: 1, 63, 64, 65, 66].

## 1. Syllabus Topics
* **Bipolar Junction Transistors (BJT):** Basic physical construction (NPN and PNP), doping profiles, region widths (emitter, base, collector), transistor action, current components in active mode ($I_E = I_B + I_C$), and minority carrier leakage currents ($I_{CBO}, I_{CEO}$)[cite: 1, 63, 65].

# UNIT IV: FIELD EFFECT TRANSISTORS (FET & MOSFET)

## 1. Syllabus Topics
* **Junction Field Effect Transistor (JFET):** Physical construction and working of N-channel and P-channel JFETs, channel conductivity, depletion layer profile under reverse bias[cite: 1, 63, 64, 65, 66].
* JFET characteristics: Drain static characteristics ($I_D$ vs. $V_{DS}$), transfer characteristics ($I_D$ vs. $V_{GS}$), Shockley's equation: $I_D = I_{DSS} \left(1 - \frac{V_{GS}}{V_P}\right)^2$, pinch-off voltage ($V_P$), and maximum drain saturation current ($I_{DSS}$)[cite: 1, 63, 64, 66].
* JFET operational parameters: Dynamic drain resistance ($r_d = \frac{\Delta V_{DS}}{\Delta I_D}$), transconductance ($g_m = \frac{\Delta I_D}{\Delta V_{GS}}$), amplification factor ($\mu = r_d \cdot g_m$)[cite: 1, 63, 64].
* JFET Configurations & Biasing: Common Source (CS), Common Drain (CD), Common Gate (CG), fixed bias, self-bias, and voltage-divider bias circuits[cite: 1, 63, 66].
* **Metal Oxide Semiconductor FET (MOSFET):** Depletion-type MOSFET (D-MOSFET) and Enhancement-type MOSFET (E-MOSFET)[cite: 1, 63, 64, 65, 66].
* Construction, operating principle, inversion layer formation, threshold voltage ($V_{TH}$), drain and transfer characteristics for N-channel and P-channel D-MOSFET and E-MOSFET[cite: 1, 63, 64, 65, 66].
* **FET Small-Signal Amplifiers:** Small-signal equivalent model of JFET/MOSFET, analysis of single-stage Common Source (CS) amplifier, phase inversion ($180^\circ$), derivation of voltage gain ($A_v = -g_m (r_d \parallel R_D)$), input resistance ($R_i$), and output resistance ($R_o$)[cite: 1, 63].
