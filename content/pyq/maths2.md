# UNIT I: FIRST-ORDER ORDINARY DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS

## 1. Syllabus Topics
* **First-Order ODEs:** Classification of differential equations, order and degree, variable separable form, reducible to separable form[cite: 1, 167, 169, 175, 183].
* **Homogeneous & Reducible Equations:** Homogeneous differential equations, equations reducible to homogeneous form ($\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{ax+by+c}{a'x+b'y+c'}$)[cite: 1, 167, 169, 171, 177, 180].
* **Exact Equations:** Exact differential equations ($M dx + N dy = 0$, condition $\frac{\partial M}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial N}{\partial x}$), integrating factors, equations reducible to exact form[cite: 1, 167, 171, 175, 177, 180, 184].
* **Linear Differential Equations:** First-order linear differential equations ($\frac{dy}{dx} + Py = Q$), equations reducible to linear form (Bernoulli's equation)[cite: 1, 169, 181, 183].
* **First-Order Higher-Degree Equations:** Equations not of first degree, equations solvable for $p$ ($p = \frac{dy}{dx}$), equations solvable for $y$, equations solvable for $x$, Clairaut's form ($y = px + f(p)$) and singular solutions[cite: 1, 167, 170, 171, 175, 177, 179, 180, 181].

# UNIT II: ORDINARY DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS OF HIGHER ORDERS

## 1. Syllabus Topics
* **Linear ODEs with Constant Coefficients:** Higher-order linear differential equations with constant coefficients, complementary functions (CF) for real, distinct, repeated, and complex roots, particular integral (PI) for $e^{ax}$, $\sin(ax)$, $\cos(ax)$, $x^m$, $e^{ax}V(x)$, and $x V(x)$[cite: 1, 167, 169, 170, 171, 175, 177, 179, 181, 183, 184].
* **Simultaneous Linear Differential Equations:** Systems of linear ODEs with constant coefficients ($\frac{dx}{dt}$ and $\frac{dy}{dt}$)[cite: 1, 167, 171, 175, 177, 180].
* **Method of Variation of Parameters:** Second-order ODEs, Wronskian determinant $W(y_1, y_2)$, formulas for complete solution[cite: 1, 169, 170, 171, 175, 177, 180, 181, 184].
* **Variable Coefficients (Cauchy-Euler & Legendre Forms):** Cauchy-Euler homogeneous equations ($x^2 D^2 + x D$), transformation $x = e^z$, Legendre linear equations ($(ax+b)^2 D^2$)[cite: 1, 170, 171, 175, 177, 179, 181].
* **Second-Order ODEs with Variable Coefficients:** Method of changing the independent variable, method of removing the first derivative (normal form)[cite: 169, 170, 171, 175, 181].

# UNIT III: SEQUENCES, SERIES & FOURIER SERIES

* **Sequences & Infinite Series:** Definition of convergence, divergence, and oscillation of series, necessary condition for convergence ($\lim u_n = 0$)[cite: 1, 167, 170, 179].

# UNIT IV: PARTIAL DIFFERENTIAL EQUATIONS

## 1. Syllabus Topics
* **PDE Formulations & First-Order Equations:** Formation of partial differential equations by elimination of arbitrary constants and arbitrary functions, classification of PDEs (linear, semi-linear, quasi-linear, non-linear)[cite: 1, 168, 169, 170, 175, 178, 181].
* **First-Order Linear & Non-Linear PDEs:** Lagrange's linear equation ($P p + Q q = R$), method of characteristics/multipliers, standard forms of non-linear first-order PDEs: $f(p, q) = 0$, $f(z, p, q) = 0$, $f(x, p) = g(y, q)$, and Clairaut's form ($z = px + qy + f(p, q)$)[cite: 1, 168, 169, 170, 171, 178, 181].
* **Homogeneous Linear PDEs of Higher Order:** Homogeneous linear equations with constant coefficients ($\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} + a\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x\partial y} + b\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} = F(x, y)$), complementary function and particular integral evaluation for $e^{ax+by}$, $\sin(ax+by)$, $\cos(ax+by)$, and $x^m y^n$[cite: 1, 168, 169, 170, 171, 175, 178, 179, 181, 183, 184].
